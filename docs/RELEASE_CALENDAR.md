## Quizzes and optional sessions

The three quizzes are **formative and unmarked (0%)**, with proposed dates of
**24 November, 4 December and 7 December 2026**. Availability and instructions are
confirmed on Canvas. Quizzes do not contribute to the final module mark.

The teaching page's **Additional sessions** are optional preparation and independent
study. They have no extra scheduled dates and do not add to the four required
